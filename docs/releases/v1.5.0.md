# Poolamkoo v1.5.0

## Loan Intelligence Layer

Added:

- Loan intelligence dashboard for cost, asset value and loan health
- Dynamic linked-asset performance tracking from investment transactions and Market Layer prices
- Conservative, balanced and growth allocation templates
- Loan Health Score with repayment, reserve, coverage and overdue signals
- Loan vs Investment comparison and early-repayment interest analysis
- Advanced single-asset and combined shock simulations
- Loan decision timeline
- Loan Intelligence Report

All allocation templates and market scenarios are analytical comparisons, not definitive financial advice. The release keeps the existing Dexie schema at version 9 and preserves current loan routes and APIs.
